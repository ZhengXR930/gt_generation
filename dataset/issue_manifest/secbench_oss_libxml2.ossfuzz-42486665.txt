Heap-use-after-free in xmlNodeDumpOutputInternal (xmlsave.c): during XInclude processing (xmlXIncludeIncludeNode in xinclude.c) namespace/node structures are freed (via xmlFreeNs/xmlFreeNode) while they are still reachable and later accessed by document-dumping functions (xmlDocContentDumpOutput / xmlDocDumpFormatMemoryEnc). This use-after-free can cause crashes and memory corruption, with potential for exploitation.
