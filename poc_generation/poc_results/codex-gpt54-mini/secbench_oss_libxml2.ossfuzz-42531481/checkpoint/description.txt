Issue description:
Heap-use-after-free in libxml2: during entity/reference parsing (xmlParseReference in parser.c), xmlStaticCopyNode (tree.c) invoked via xmlDocCopyNode reads node/namespace data that has already been freed. This results in reads from freed heap memory, leading to crashes and potential memory-corruption-based exploitation.
