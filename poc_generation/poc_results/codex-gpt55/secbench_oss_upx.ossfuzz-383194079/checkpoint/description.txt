Issue description:
Heap-buffer-overflow (out-of-bounds read) in PackMachBase::canUnpack (p_mach.cpp) via get_ne32/get_le32 (bele.h). While iterating Mach-O load commands (including fat/32-bit paths), a malformed or too-short command can cause a 4-byte read past the allocated buffer, leading to crashes and possible disclosure of heap memory.
