Issue description:
A heap use after free read occurs during X.509 certificate parsing when a malformed Subject Alternative Name extension triggers cleanup of the allocated `subject_alt_name` sequence. The cleanup frees the linked list entries but does not clear the `subject_alt_name->next` pointer, so later certificate cleanup may traverse this dangling pointer and read freed heap memory.
