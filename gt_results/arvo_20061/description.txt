Issue description:
A heap-use-after-free occurs during cleanup of a decoded variant. When decoding fails, the error path frees the just-allocated variant body but leaves the owning variant's fields unchanged, so later generic cleanup reuses a dangling pointer to a freed LocalizedText, leading to a read of freed memory.
