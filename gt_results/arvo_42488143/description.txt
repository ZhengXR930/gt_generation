Issue description:
A heap-use-after-free occurs in flb_parser_create() when a time_offset parse failure triggers flb_parser_destroy(p), which frees the decoder list while it still aliases caller-owned state. Later, flb_parser_decoder_list_destroy() iterates the freed list head, reading stale pointers and causing a heap-use-after-free read.
