Issue description:
A heap buffer over-read occurs while HDF5 decodes a legacy superblock whose address widths make the cached root-entry fields larger than the available scratch area. The root-entry decoder consumes two addresses without checking the remaining image length, allowing address decoding to continue past the loaded superblock buffer.
