Issue description:
A use-of-uninitialized-value occurs in the decompressor when a zero-distance back-reference is not rejected by the non-wrapping output buffer guard. This leads to a self-referential copy from the current output position, and the resulting poisoned Adler32 checksum is later compared, triggering the uninitialized read.
