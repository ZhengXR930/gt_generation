Issue description:
A use-after-free occurs in word translation when TranslateWord2 passes translator2 as the active translator and nested translation paths such as TranslateWord3, SpeakIndividualLetters, or TranslateLetter reassign translator2 with SetTranslator2. This can free the translator object still in use through the tr parameter, leaving subsequent translation operations to access freed memory.
