Issue description:
A heap-use-after-free occurs in the barcode extraction routine when the scan count exceeds the crop height. The clamp condition is reversed, so `nscans` remains larger than the image height. The raster-averaging loop then reads bytes from rows beyond the valid range, landing in a freed heap region of a temporary PIX.
