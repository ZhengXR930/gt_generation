Issue description:
Heap-buffer-overflow (out-of-bounds read) in PackLinuxElf32::elf_lookup (src/p_lx_elf.cpp). While walking ELF DT_HASH chains to resolve symbols the code can index past the end of the chains table and invoke a 32-bit read (via the get32/get_le32 path), causing a 4-byte heap OOB read. A malformed or malicious DT_HASH can therefore trigger crashes and potential information disclosure.
