Issue description:
A use-after-free occurs in word translation when TranslateWord2 passes translator2 as the active translator to TranslateWord. During nested calls such as TranslateWord3, SpeakIndividualLetters, or TranslateLetter, SetTranslator2 may be invoked again and free that same translator object while it is still in use.
