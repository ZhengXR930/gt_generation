Issue description:
A heap buffer overflow occurs during fast-interpreter preparation of WebAssembly bytecode with typed block parameters. The block-parameter path subtracts the full width of parsed values from the frame-offset stack without checking its lower bound, then writes through the underflowed frame-offset pointer.
