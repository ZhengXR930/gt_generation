Issue description:
A heap-use-after-free occurs in the regex compiler's empty-look-behind optimization. When a look-behind is proven to match the empty string, the optimization frees the look-behind body without checking for back-referenced capture groups, leaving a dangling pointer that is later dereferenced during node length calculation.
