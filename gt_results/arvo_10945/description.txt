Issue description:
The generic key/mask parser treats 'ufid:' as a successful parse in all contexts, returning its length without appending any key or mask attribute. This leaves the caller's mask buffer uninitialized, and a subsequent size read on the missing attribute triggers a use-of-uninitialized-value.
