Issue description:
Heap-buffer-overflow in PackLinuxElf64::unpack (p_lx_elf.cpp). While unpacking ELF data, N_BELE_RTP::LEPolicy::get64 / get_le64 (bele_policy.h / bele.h) can read an 8-byte little-endian value past the end of a MemBuffer allocation, due to insufficient bounds validation of ELF header/segment data. This out-of-bounds read can cause crashes and may lead to information disclosure when processing malformed ELF files.
