Issue description:
An invalid-free in libxml2's parser (parser.c) causes xmlFreeNodeList/xmlFreeNode to attempt to free an address that was not obtained from malloc. The error occurs during reference/balanced-chunk parsing (xmlParseReference / xmlParseBalancedChunkMemoryInternal), leading to crashes and potential heap corruption when processing crafted XML (e.g., schema) inputs.
