Issue description:
A heap use-after-free occurs during mbregex compilation of patterns that combine a subexpression call with look-behind simplification. Simplifying a zero-length look-behind can free a capture-group syntax-tree node while a call node still retains its body pointer, and the optimizer later follows that stale pointer.
