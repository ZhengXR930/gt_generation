Issue description:
The version-scheme reader in the uswid parser validates only that the CBOR item is an unsigned integer, but does not check its width. As a result, a one-byte integer is passed to cbor_get_uint16, which reads two bytes from the item's data buffer, causing a heap-buffer-overflow.
