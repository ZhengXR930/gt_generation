Issue description:
A heap-buffer-overflow occurs in HarfBuzz's sanitizer when a malformed font is processed. On the second sanitize pass, the code eagerly evaluates `this+typeList` before validating that the `ResourceMap` pointer is in-bounds, leading to an out-of-bounds read of 1 byte during a big-endian 16-bit load.
