Issue description:
The lazy-chunk decompression path trusts the chunk header's cbytes field without verifying that the claimed chunk fits within the frame. This allows an attacker-controlled header to cause a memcpy from a dictionary pointer that extends beyond the input buffer, resulting in a heap-buffer-overflow read.
