Issue description:
A heap-buffer-overflow occurs in HarfBuzz's sanitizer when processing a malformed dfont. The first `sfnt` type's resource-array offset is sanitized through a null-enabled `OffsetTo`, so when deep validation fails, the offset is zeroed and the blob is accepted. This leads to an out-of-bounds read of 1 byte when loading the table count from a forged header.
