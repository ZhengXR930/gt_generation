Issue description:
A heap buffer overflow occurs while OpenJPEG records tile-part positions during JPEG 2000 tile-header decoding. A later tile-part number can exceed the number of entries allocated from the tile-length marker, but the decoder uses that number to update the tile-part index without validating the index bound.
