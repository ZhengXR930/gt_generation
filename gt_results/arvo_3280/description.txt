Issue description:
In OpenThread's IPv6 send path, the Network Data Leader's `AddHasRoute` function copies a `HasRouteEntry` from a HasRoute TLV without verifying that the TLV contains at least one entry. The missing precondition makes the copy read beyond the stack-allocated TLV region, causing a stack buffer over-read.
