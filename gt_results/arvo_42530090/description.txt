Issue description:
An out-of-bounds write occurs during GRE layer processing when IPv4 or IPv6 parsing constructs a GREv0 or GREv1 layer from payload data without verifying that the buffer is at least the size of the corresponding GRE header. This allows later field calculation in `computeCalculateFields()` to access and write GRE header fields in an undersized packet buffer.
