Issue description:
A use-after-free occurs in the `hb_auto_t` wrapper when it is constructed from a templated argument that is not restricted to pointers or references. The unconstrained constructor can make an unintended copy instead of preserving reference semantics, leaving later operations to access storage that has already been released.
